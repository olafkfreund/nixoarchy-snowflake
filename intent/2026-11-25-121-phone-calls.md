# Intent: place phone calls from Nixi, with contact names on the handset

## Problem

Nixi can answer, reject, mute and hang up a phone call, but it cannot start
one.

- **No dial.** `app/hfp.py` talks to PipeWire's Bluetooth telephony
  service (`org.pipewire.Telephony`) and implements answer, hangup, mute
  and volume. Nothing calls the phone's dial method. On p620 the Pixel's
  gateway (`/org/pipewire/Telephony/ag1`) does offer one
  (`AudioGateway1.Dial`).
- **No contacts.** Nixi has no phonebook. The only names it sees are
  SMS thread names and caller ID.
- **The handset shows a name only when the call carries one.** The
  "mobile phone" display (`HS` in `app/static/index.html`, fed by
  `Call.sync` in `app/server.py:8155-8200`) shows `who`, which is Flux's
  caller name or the Bluetooth call's `Name`, and otherwise the bare
  number. Without Flux, or when the phone sends only a number, the owner
  sees digits instead of who is calling.
- **No outgoing call states.** `Call.sync` treats every call that is not
  ringing as active (`:8159`). An outgoing call would start its timer
  before it is answered, and would have no hang-up button while dialing.
- **No voice or click path.** There is no "call Anna" action, no way to
  choose between two Annas or between mobile and work, and no
  confirmation step.

## Proposed outcome

- **Contact names on the handset.** Nixi can look up the Pixel's contacts.
  For any call, incoming or outgoing, it shows the contact's name and the
  number's label (mobile, work) on the existing handset display. If the
  phone already sent a name, that name is kept. If the contact is unknown,
  the display shows the number, as it does today.
- **"Call Anna" works.** By voice or by typing, the owner names a contact
  or a number. Nixi finds the matches. When several people or numbers
  match, it shows a card to pick from, and then always a confirmation
  card with the name, the label and the number. A real click places the
  call on the phone.
- **Calls use the host's mic and speaker** over Bluetooth, as answered
  calls already do. Nixi goes quiet and frees the mic during the call.
- **The handset follows an outgoing call:** "Calling…" with hang-up while
  it rings, then the usual in-call controls and timer once answered.
- **Clear messages when it can't work**, separately for each cause:
  - the phone is not connected over Bluetooth;
  - contact sharing is off on the phone;
  - another call is in progress;
  - the number is not allowed.

## Affected users and systems

- `app/hfp.py` (dial, gateway choice), a new phonebook module, and
  `app/server.py` (the `Call` state machine, a voice/quick action, a dial
  endpoint, contact lookup for the handset).
- `app/quick.py` (the "call …" phrase), `app/static/index.html` (the
  pick and confirm cards and the handset's dialing state), and the locale
  files under `app/lang/`.
- Tests under `tests/`, using synthetic contacts only.
- Hosts: p620 and razer via the usual `nixi-companion` bump. The Pixel 11
  Pro XL is paired over Bluetooth. p510 is not touched without the
  owner's OK.
- Flux and the Android app are not changed in this issue.

## Constraints

- **Voice never dials on its own.** Every call needs a real click on the
  confirmation card, even when there is a single match. The confirmation
  is single-use, tied to that number and that phone, and works only while
  the desktop is unlocked.
- **No second call while one is up.** Dialing then could put the first
  call on hold.
- **Service and MMI codes are rejected**, such as `*#06#` and USSD
  strings, along with post-dial strings. Ordinary numbers and a leading
  `+` are allowed.
- **No double dial.** If it is unclear whether a dial went through, Nixi
  waits for the call state and does not retry.
- **Contact data stays private:**
  - it is fetched over the local Bluetooth link only;
  - it is held in memory for a short time and never written to disk,
    except a phonebook transfer file in a private runtime directory,
    deleted at once;
  - it is never logged, never put in history, and never sent to the AI
    model or any cloud service;
  - lookups for the handset and for "call …" stay on the local path.
- Tests use made-up contacts and simulated Bluetooth events. They never
  touch the real phone, Bluetooth, adb, or the owner's live config
  (`tests/nixi_env.py`).
- Manual hardware checks call only a harmless number, such as voicemail,
  and only with the owner present.

## Decisions (approved 2026-11-25)

1. Contacts come from Bluetooth phonebook access (PBAP) through `obexd`.
   The owner turns on "Contact sharing" for p620 in the Pixel's Bluetooth
   settings. A Flux `contacts.search` is a later fallback, only if PBAP
   proves unreliable.
2. The phonebook is pulled on first need, kept in memory for 10 minutes,
   and dropped when the phone disconnects or Phone is turned off.
3. With several phones on Bluetooth, the Flux-paired device dials;
   otherwise Nixi asks with the existing phone picker.
4. Typed numbers without a contact are allowed, through the same
   confirmation card.
