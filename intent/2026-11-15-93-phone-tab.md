# Intent: Settings gets a Phone tab of its own

## Problem

The Phone (Flux) switch from #231 sits in Settings → Look (`app/static/index.html:936-937`), between "Say out loud" and
Mail. Look is mostly about how Nixi appears and what he wears. The phone is a separate feature, so it's hard to find
there.

The phone feature already covers finding the phone, sending to it, battery, calls and messages (#231), and its settings
will grow. Each new one would make Look longer and more mixed.

## Proposed outcome

- Settings has a sixth tab, **Phone**, with its own icon, next to the others.
- The whole Phone row (`#phoneRow`, holding `#phoneSw` and `#phoneStat`) moves there, with its IDs, unchanged in what
  it does.
- Look no longer shows the Phone row.
- The six tabs fit in the Settings window at its smallest width (420 px): no label is clipped or wraps, and no tab
  overflows the bar. With six tabs each gets about 65 px, which leaves about 46 px for "Plugins" next to its icon, so
  this is checked by a test, not only by eye.
- The sliding indicator sits under whichever tab is selected, Phone included, at the smallest width and after a
  reopen.
- The keyboard behaviour stays the same: Left and Right wrap, Home and End jump, and the selected tab gets the focus,
  with ARIA links and the roving tabindex on the new tab too.
- A remembered tab (`localStorage` `nixi.tab`) still opens correctly, `phone` becomes a valid value, and an unknown
  value still falls back to Look.

## Affected users and systems

- `app/static/index.html`: the tab bar (`:879-883`) and maybe its CSS (`:613-620`) if six tabs don't fit, the panels
  (from `:888`), `TABS` (`:4040`), and the Phone row (`:936-937`).
- `tests/settings_fit.mjs`:
  - section 8 checks exactly five tabs (`:366-390`)
  - section 9 hard-codes five tabindex states and expects Left from More to reach Plugins (`:393-413`)
  - section 10 covers the remembered tab (`:415` on)

  All three change. No other test uses the Phone row (a grep for `phoneRow`, `phoneSw` and `phoneStat` finds none).
- The p620 desktop (WebKitGTK), where the tab bar's fit is judged by eye after a deploy with the owner's OK.

## Constraints

- No change to what the Phone switch does, its setting (`phone_on`), or the server.
- Tab labels stay plain HTML, like the other five (they aren't in `app/lang`).
- Tests use headless Chromium with `--disable-gpu`, started by the test. Test servers use `nixi_env`.

## Open questions

- **Where does the tab go?** After Brain and before Plugins, or after Plugins and before More? The proposal is
  **after Plugins, before More**, so More stays last as the catch-all.
- **What goes in the tab now?** The proposal is only the switch and its status. The other phone settings come with
  their own issues as the feature grows.
- **Should Mail move with it** (into a "Phone & mail" tab, or its own tab)? The proposal is no, so this issue stays
  small.
