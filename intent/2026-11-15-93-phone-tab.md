# Intent: Settings gets a Phone tab of its own

## Problem

The Phone (Flux) switch from #231 sits in Settings → Look (`app/static/index.html:936-937`), between "Say out loud" and
Mail. Look is about how Nixi appears and what he wears. The phone is a separate feature, so it's hard to find there.

The phone feature will also grow, with devices, battery, calls and messages. Each new setting would make Look longer
and more mixed.

## Proposed outcome

- Settings has a sixth tab, **Phone**, with its own icon, next to the others.
- The Phone (Flux) switch and its status line (`#phoneSw`, `#phoneStat`) move there, unchanged in what they do.
- Look no longer shows the Phone row.
- The tabs still fit in the Settings window at its smallest width (420 px), and the keyboard behaviour stays the
  same: Left and Right wrap, Home and End jump, and the selected tab gets the focus (`settings_fit` section 9).
- A remembered tab (`localStorage` `nixi.tab`) still opens correctly, and `phone` becomes a valid value.

## Affected users and systems

- `app/static/index.html`: the tab bar (`:879-883`), the panels (`:888-1025`), `TABS` (`:4040`), and the Phone row.
- `tests/settings_fit.mjs`: section 8 checks exactly five tabs (`:366-390`). It must change to six.
- The p620 desktop (WebKitGTK), where the tab bar's fit is judged by eye after a deploy with the owner's OK.

## Constraints

- No change to what the Phone switch does, its setting (`phone_on`), or the server.
- Tab labels stay plain HTML, like the other five (they aren't in `app/lang`).
- Tests use headless Chromium with `--disable-gpu`, started by the test. Test servers use `nixi_env`.
- The Mail row stays in Look in this issue.

## Open questions

- **Where does the tab go?** After Brain and before Plugins, or after Plugins and before More? The proposal is
  **after Plugins, before More**, so More stays last as the catch-all.
- **What goes in the tab now?** The proposal is only the switch and its status. The other phone settings come with
  their own issues as the feature grows.
- **Should Mail move with it** (into a "Phone & mail" tab, or its own tab)? The proposal is no, so this issue stays
  small.
