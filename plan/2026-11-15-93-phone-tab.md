# Plan: Settings gets a Phone tab of its own

## Summary of the approved spec

- **A sixth tab, Phone,** goes between Plugins and More, with a phone icon. Its panel goes between the Plugins and
  More panels. The whole `#phoneRow` (with `#phoneSw` and `#phoneStat`) moves there from Look unchanged, IDs
  included. `TABS` gets `'phone'` in the same order as the buttons, because the keyboard handler (`:4078-4084`) steps
  through `TABS`.
- **Room at narrow widths:** `#tabs{container-type:inline-size}` and
  `@container (width < 414px){ #tabs button svg{display:none} }`.
  - A container query measures the content box, which is the bar minus its 6 px of padding.
  - So icons show from a 446 px Settings window up (a 420 px bar, 69 px tabs, 4 px spare for "Plugins" with DejaVu)
    and hide below that.
- **The indicator follows a Settings resize:** `setResize`'s frame callback also calls `moveInd()`.
- **Tests:** `settings_fit` sections 8, 9 and 10 go to six tabs, plus a new fit section after 10 (Step 2 has the
  details).
- **Measured** (headless Chromium, six tabs, "Plugins" spare): with Liberation, 6.3 px at 420 and 29.6 at 560. With
  DejaVu, 0 at 420 without the fix.
- **Not changed:** the Phone switch's behaviour and setting (`phone_on`), the server, Mail (it stays in Look), the
  tab labels (plain HTML, not in `app/lang`).
