# Plan: place phone calls from Nixi, with contact names on the handset

Self-contained. It carries every approved intent and spec decision.

## Approved decisions

- **D1. Contacts** come from Bluetooth PBAP through `obexd` (session bus
  `org.bluez.obex`). The owner turns on Contact sharing per host. A Flux
  `contacts.search` is a later fallback only.
- **D2. The phonebook cache** is per Bluetooth address, in memory, for
  10 minutes. Failures are cached with a 5-minute backoff. It is dropped,
  and the generation bumped, on gateway loss, Phone off and lock. A worker
  from an older generation discards its result.
- **D3. Which phone dials:**
  - one HFP gateway is used;
  - several show a phone pick card by BlueZ alias, read from the system
    bus, with the last 4 address characters when aliases repeat;
  - none: "not connected over Bluetooth".
  This replaces the intent's "Flux-paired" rule, as approved in the
  spec. Caller names come only from the phonebook of the call's own
  gateway.
- **D4. Typed numbers** are allowed through the same confirm card.
- **D5. Privacy:**
  - phonebook names are display-only;
  - the ring announcement, missed-call cards and anything spoken get only
    `phone_who`, the name the phone sent;
  - replies never repeat the target or a full number;
  - nothing logs names or numbers;
  - the vCard file lives only in a `0700` runtime directory, deleted in
    `finally`, and stale ones are removed at startup.
- **D6. Safety:**
  - only a trusted pointer click on the confirm card dials, never voice
    and never Enter or Space;
  - `PHONE_CALL` tokens are bound to the request id, `PHONE_GEN` and a
    60 s expiry, and a new request replaces the old;
  - `DIAL_PENDING` blocks any second dial until HFP shows the call, or
    30 s pass. An `unknown` dial result keeps it;
  - there is no dial while a call is showing or in progress.
- **D7. Allowed numbers:**
  - normalise by stripping `tel:`, Unicode spaces and dashes, dots and
    brackets;
  - allow `+?[0-9]{6,15}`;
  - refuse `* # , ; p w` (`phone.call_not_allowed`) and 1 to 5 digits
    (`phone.call_short`);
  - the same rule applies to contact numbers.
- **D8. Lock order** is `Call.lock` before `PHONE_LOCK`, always. The
  dial endpoint checks call state before it takes `PHONE_LOCK`.

## Steps

1. **`app/pbap.py` (new) and `tests/test_pbap.py` (new).** Codex.
   - The `Pbap` class, with an injectable connection and Gio, as `Hfp`
     takes:
     - `start()`, which removes stale `nixi-pbap-*` directories;
     - `state(address)`, `name_for(address, number)`,
       `search(address, query)`;
     - `ensure(address, on_ready)`, which starts at most one worker per
       address;
     - `drop(address=None)`, which bumps the generation.
   - The load, the errors and the parsing follow spec Design 1 exactly:
     - **Load:** subscribe before `PullAll`; honour an already-terminal
       `Status`; 20 s timeout, then `Transfer1.Cancel()`; read only after
       `complete`; `finally` cleanup, then `RemoveSession`.
     - **Errors:** a not-authorised or forbidden `CreateSession`, or a
       refused `Select`/`PullAll`, gives `sharing`. Any other
       `CreateSession` failure gives `unreachable`. A transfer error or
       timeout gives `failed`.
     - **Parsing:** `FN`, falling back to `N`. `TEL` labels map to
       mobile, home, work or other. Each number gets a normalised key,
       and numbers failing D7 are marked not dialable.
     - `name_for` returns an exact key, or a unique last-8-digit match,
       and otherwise `None`.
     - `search` is accent-insensitive (exact, then word-prefix, then
       substring), returns at most 8 people, and only dialable numbers.
   - A module-level `allowed_number(raw)` implements D7. Steps 4 and 5
     use it.
   - Tests use synthetic vCards and a fake D-Bus only. Write every case
     listed under `tests/test_pbap.py` in the spec's Verification.
   - Check: `nix develop -c python3 -B -m unittest discover -s tests -p 'test_pbap.py'`.
   - Traps:
     - never touch the real `obexd`, Bluetooth or contacts;
     - no logging of names or numbers;
     - the target path given to `PullAll` must be absolute.

2. **`app/hfp.py`: `dial()` and `gateway_list()`; `tests/test_hfp.py`.**
   Codex.
   - `dial(ag_path, number)`:
     - calls `AudioGateway1.Dial` with `GLib.Variant("(s)", (number,))`
       on `ag_path`, through the connection's `call_sync` (as `_call`
       does), not `_action`;
     - refuses with `busy` when `any_call()`, and with `gone` when the
       path is not in `self.gateways`;
     - maps a timeout or lost reply to `unknown`;
     - returns `{"ok": True}` on success.
   - `gateway_list()`:
     - returns `[(path, address, alias)]`;
     - takes the alias from the system bus BlueZ `ObjectManager`, the
       `org.bluez.Device1` whose `Address` matches, injectable for tests,
       falling back to the address;
     - appends the last 4 address characters when two aliases are equal.
   - Tests: every case listed under `tests/test_hfp*.py` in the spec's
     Verification.
   - Check: `… -p 'test_hfp.py'`.
   - Traps: the existing tests inject `conn`/`gio`/`glib` (`hfp.py:21`),
     so follow that pattern.

3. **`app/server.py` `Call` (`:8008-8270`): identity and outgoing states;
   `tests/test_call.py`.** Coder.
   - **Identity:**
     - the row gets `phone_who` (today's `who`) and a display `who`;
     - lookup only with an HFP leg, and only with `h["number"]`, through
       `Pbap.name_for(address, …)`, with the address from
       `Hfp.gateways[h["ag"]]`;
     - it applies when `phone_who` is empty or a bare number (digits only
       after stripping `+`, spaces, dashes, dots and brackets);
     - a real `phone_who` is kept, with `label` added only on a unique
       match;
     - a cold cache calls `Pbap.ensure(address, on_ready=Call.sync)`.
       `on_ready` runs only on a `ready` load of the same generation.
   - **`label`:** add it to the key lists in `_emit` (`:8032`) and
     `current_event` (`:8063`).
   - **Privacy:** `on_missed` (`:8135`) and the ring announcement
     (`phone_on_call`, around `:8344`) get `phone_who`.
   - **Outgoing states,** for HFP calls only:
     - `dialing`/`alerting` give phase `dialing`, path `host`, keys
       `["hangup"]` and `answered_at` `None`, and set `hfp_outgoing`;
     - `active` only on HFP `active`;
     - the `ended` rule (`:8128-8129`) also covers `old["phase"] ==
       "dialing"`, for the same HFP path, going `disconnected`;
     - `second` still empties the keys;
     - a Flux-only `talking` call is unchanged.
   - Wire `Pbap` like `Hfp`: a module instance started with the server,
     and `Pbap.drop()` when Phone is turned off, on lock, and for an
     address when its gateway leaves `Hfp.gateways`.
   - Tests: every `Call.sync` case listed in the spec's Verification.
     `test_call.py` already restores `Ambient.phone_call`; keep that.
   - Check: `… -p 'test_call.py'`, then `… -p 'test_phone*.py'`.
   - Traps:
     - `Call.sync` holds `Call.lock` while it calls `on_missed`, which
       takes `PHONE_LOCK`, so never take `Call.lock` inside `PHONE_LOCK`
       (D8);
     - tests must not import the server under the real HOME (use
       `nixi_env`).

4. **`app/quick.py`, `phone_quick` and locale: the "call …" phrase.**
   Coder.
   - **`quick.py`:**
     - adds `_phone_call(t)`, which returns
       `{"phone": {"do": "call", "target": x}}` per spec Design 6;

       number-like, or one to four words;
     - the `phone_call_stop` list excludes `the/that/this/a/an/it/me/us/them`

       and after anything that owns "call me at 5" (the timer), so those
       keep winning;
     - it has no side effects.
   - **`phone_quick`:**
     - handles `do == "call"` first, before the `Phone.connected` gate,
       and never returns `None` for it;
     - it walks the spec Design 6 table in order: Phone off, locked,
       busy (a call visible, `Hfp.any_call()` or `DIAL_PENDING`), no
       gateway, several gateways, number not allowed or short, allowed
       number, name with each cache state, 0, 1 or many matches;
     - the cards come from Step 5's helpers.
   - **Locale:** add every key in spec Design 9 to `en.json`, `nb.json`

   - Tests:
     - every row of the Design 6 table;
     - "call that function", "call me at 5" and "ring my phone" fall
       through as today;
     - "call Anna", "dial *#06#" and "call 112" are handled;
     - one `nb` and one `pl` phrase, and the English fallback;
     - no reply contains the target or a full number.
   - Check: `… -p 'test_quick*.py'`, `… -p 'test_phone*.py'`, and

   - Traps: the existing `_phone_phrase` character class

5. **`app/server.py`: request state, cards, endpoints.** Coder.
   - **State, under `PHONE_LOCK`:**
     - `PHONE_CALL = {id, gen, gateway_path, address, created, rows: {token: (name, label, number)}, confirm}`;
     - `DIAL_PENDING = {gateway_path, number, since}`, cleared when HFP
       shows a call on that gateway, after 30 s, or at once on `busy`
       or `gone`;
     - `PHONE_GEN` is bumped where SMS drafts already bump it, and on
       gateway loss.
   - **Cards:** helpers that put `phone_call_gateway`, `phone_call_pick`
     and `phone_call_confirm` stages, with display fields plus opaque
     tokens. Add the three kinds to `phone_on_screen()` (`:7802`), and
     clear them on lock, Phone off, gateway loss and replacement.
   - **Endpoints,** next to `/api/phone/send` (`:10437`). Each requires
     `click_ok`, or returns 403:
     - `/api/phone/call-gateway {token}`;
     - `/api/phone/call-pick {token}`, which only makes the confirm;
     - `/api/phone/dial {token}`, in three parts:
       1. check `Call.visible()` and `Hfp.any_call()` first, without
          `PHONE_LOCK`;
       2. under `PHONE_LOCK`, validate the token, expiry, `PHONE_GEN`,
          Phone on, unlocked, the gateway present with the same address,
          and no `DIAL_PENDING`; then consume and set `DIAL_PENDING`;
       3. call `Hfp.dial` once, outside the lock.
       A failure never brings the confirm back.
     - Replies are `{"ok", "say"}`, with `phone.call_failed` or
       `phone.call_stale`.
   - **Tests:** every endpoint case listed in the spec's Verification,
     including:
     - two confirms clicked before the first HFP event give one dial;
     - an `unknown` result keeps `DIAL_PENDING`;
     - a missed call landing during `/api/phone/dial` causes no deadlock
       (two threads with a timeout assert).
   - Check: `… -p 'test_phone*.py'`, `… -p 'test_call.py'`.
   - Traps: keep `phone_send`'s structure as the model; never hold
     `PHONE_LOCK` while calling into `Call` or `Hfp.dial` (D8).
